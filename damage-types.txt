ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN: DAMAGE TYPES, AFFINITIES AND STATUS EFFECTS
(from the Fextralife Nightreign wiki: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/)

DAMAGE TYPES
Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Damage_Types
There are 8 damage types in two groups.
Physical: Standard (effective against most enemies), Strike (blunt weapons like hammers and maces), Slash (sharp weapons like katanas and daggers), Pierce (thrusting weapons like spears and lances).
Elemental, also called affinities: Magic, Fire, Lightning, Holy.

WHAT AFFINITY MEANS
Sources: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Improved_Affinity_Attack_Power, https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Improved_Affinity_Damage_Negation, https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Weak_Affinity_Reward, https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Status_Effects
Affinity is the umbrella word for the four elemental damage types (magic, fire, lightning, holy). It is the counterpart to physical damage.
- Relic and dormant power effects: Improved Affinity Attack Power raises your magic, fire, lightning and holy attacks. Improved Affinity Damage Negation reduces the magic, fire, lightning and holy damage you take. The Physical versions of these effects do the same for physical damage.
- Weapons: an affinity weapon deals elemental damage. A Standard weapon deals physical damage. Weapon passives like Add Fire to Weapon, Add Holy to Weapon, Add Magic to Weapon and Add Lightning to Weapon give a weapon extra elemental damage.
- Fort and Great Church bosses also have an affinity (for example Fire, Sacred, Magic or Cold), which changes the reward you get for beating them.

STATUS EFFECTS
Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Status_Effects
Status effects are conditions that build up on a target with repeated hits and trigger when the buildup meter fills. They hit players, enemies and bosses alike.
Poison: continuous damage over time.
Scarlet Rot: continuous damage over time.
Blood Loss: a burst of damage that is bigger the higher the target's max HP.
Frostbite: a burst of damage that is bigger the higher the target's max HP.
Sleep: the target loses FP and is left highly vulnerable.
Madness: large loss of HP and FP.
Death Blight: the player dies.
Weapon passives that add status buildup: Attacks Inflict Poison, Attacks Inflict Rot, Attacks Inflict Blood Loss, Attacks Inflict Sleep, Attacks Release Mist of Frost. Some passives add buildup when you are below max HP (Poison Buildup When Below Max HP, Rot Buildup When Below Max HP).
Protecting yourself: resistance passives like Improved Poison Resistance, Improved Rot Resistance, Improved Blood Loss Resistance, Improved Frost Resistance, Improved Sleep Resistance, Improved Madness Resistance and Improved Deathblight Resistance (+57, +75 or +112), or All Resistance Up, make status effects take longer to build up on you.

AFFINITY VS STATUS EFFECT: THE DIFFERENCE
Sources: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Damage_Types, https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Status_Effects, https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Libra_Creature_of_Night
- What it is: an affinity is a type of damage (magic, fire, lightning, holy). A status effect is a meter that fills up with hits and does something when it is full (poison, scarlet rot, blood loss, frostbite, sleep, madness, death blight).
- When it works: affinity damage lands on every hit. A status effect does nothing until its meter is full, then it triggers as a burst (blood loss, frostbite), damage over time (poison, scarlet rot) or a condition (sleep, madness).
- On a boss page: affinities appear under Damage Negation, which is how much of that damage the boss blocks. Status effects appear under Resistances, which is how much buildup is needed to trigger them.
- On weapons: Add Fire to Weapon is an affinity. Attacks Inflict Poison is a status effect.
- Defending: Affinity Damage Negation reduces elemental damage you take. Resistance effects slow down status buildup on you.
- Example: Libra takes extra Holy damage (Holy negation -35), which is an affinity weakness and pays off on every hit. Libra also has low Poison resistance (154), which is a status weakness: poison triggers quickly, then damages it over time.

READING A BOSS PAGE
Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Libra_Creature_of_Night
Weak to: what works well. It can list affinities (like Holy) and status effects (like Poison).
Stronger VS: damage types the boss resists. Avoid these.
Damage Negation: how much of each damage type the boss blocks. Higher numbers block more. A negative number means the boss takes extra damage. The order on the wiki is Standard, Slash, Strike, Pierce, Magic, Fire, Lightning, Holy.
Resistances: how hard each status effect is to trigger. Higher numbers are harder. The order is Poison, Scarlet Rot, Blood Loss, Frostbite, Sleep, Madness. A dash means no value is listed (usually immune).

Example, Libra, Creature of Night:
Negation: Standard 0, Slash -10, Strike 0, Pierce 0, Magic 20, Fire -20, Lightning 0, Holy -35.
Resistances: Poison 154, Scarlet Rot 154, Blood Loss 252, Frostbite 252, Sleep -, Madness 154.
So Holy does the most extra damage, Fire also does extra damage, and Magic is the worst choice. Poison, Scarlet Rot and Madness trigger more easily than Blood Loss or Frostbite. The wiki lists Libra as weak to Holy, Poison, Scarlet Rot and Madness, and stronger vs Magic.

SPECIAL BUFFS (the wiki lists these as other status effects; they are buffs you receive, not conditions you inflict)
Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Status_Effects
Favor of the Mountaintop: halves frostbite damage and frost buildup on you. Frostbite triggering nearby raises your damage 1.15x for 20 seconds. Get it from the blue crystals at the top of the Mountaintop.
Favor of the Rotted Woods: makes you immune to scarlet rot and raises max HP 1.25x. Attacking after taking damage heals you for a short time.
Favor of the Great Hollow: breaks the crystal curse. When your Sacred Flasks run out it fills your Art gauge, with a 30 second cooldown.
Favor of Noklateo the Shrouded City: once, you get up from near-death on your own, then get 1.3x HP, FP and stamina for 40 seconds, 5% HP regen per second for 10 seconds, and about 3x faster ultimate art charge for 10 seconds.
Favor of the Night: permanently faster Ultimate Art gauge charging.
Power to Balance the World: sometimes you get up from near-death on your own, then deal 1.1x damage for 45 seconds.
Demon's Plating: more attack power the more runes you hold (1.2x at 50k runes up to 3x at 2 million).
Traces of Grace-Given Lord: each new Site of Grace found raises attack power 1.02x, stacking up to 10 times.
Unifying Fate: healing with a flask also restores 20% FP.
Cold Mirage: turns you invisible and immune to attacks for a short time at low HP.
Beast's Hunt: successive attacks restore stamina and boost stamina regen for you and nearby allies.
Integration of Intelligence: a chance to get 10,000 runes from killing enemies.
False Vision of a Great Rune: faster Ultimate Art charging and 2000 bonus murk after the match.

SPECIAL WEAKNESSES
Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Special_Weaknesses
Some enemy types take bonus damage from specific weapons, spells and items:
Falling Stars (Fallingstar Beasts, Stoneskin Lords, Maris): star weapons, e.g. Starscourge Greatsword +30%.
Those Who Live in Death (skeletons, Death Rite Birds, Gravebirds): Holy damage, e.g. Golden Epitaph +30%, Litany of Proper Death +900%, Holy Water Pot +400%.
Dragons (including Adel): dragon weapons and items, e.g. Dragon King's Cragblade +30%, Dragonwound Grease +30%.
Caligo counts as an Ancient Dragon: dragon-slaying weapons and items (Dragon King's Cragblade +15%, Dragonscale Blade +10%, Bolt of Gransax +10%, Dragonwound Grease +20%) do bonus damage to it, about half the bonus they do to normal dragons.
